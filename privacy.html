Lilli: Clean Sweep (the “App”) is a household chore-tracking game for families and roommates, operated by Dustin Ellsworth (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs are used in the App.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not use your information to build advertising profiles.

Children

Accounts are for adults (18 and over). A parent or guardian can add profiles for their children inside their own household. A child profile is just a first name, a color and a points total; children do not create accounts, sign in, or enter any other information. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A parent can remove a child profile at any time, or delete the whole account (see below). If you believe a child has provided information to us directly, contact us and we will delete it.

Service providers

We use these providers to operate the App. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

  • Google – Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Sign-In (privacy policy).
  • Apple – Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and App Store billing for Lilli Pro (privacy policy).

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the App: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your cloud household data and your account, removes the data stored on your device by the App, and, if you signed in with Apple, revokes the App’s Sign in with Apple access. You can also email us at dustin.t.ellsworth@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. Deleting the App from your device removes locally stored data but not a cloud account; delete the account first if you want everything removed. Cancelling Lilli Pro is done through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us at dustin.t.ellsworth@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

Security

Data in transit to Google services is encrypted, and access to cloud data is restricted to your signed-in account. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the App.
