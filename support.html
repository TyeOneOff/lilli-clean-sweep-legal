Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the App, go to Settings → Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and your cloud data and clears data stored on your device. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

What is Lilli Pro?

Lilli Pro ($1.99/month, auto-renewing) turns on Cloud Sync, so your household is saved to your account and available on all your devices. Everything else in the App is free.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

To restore, open Settings → Lilli Pro → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID. To cancel, use your device’s Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Lilli Pro. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why isn’t my data showing on a second device?

Cloud Sync must be on (it’s part of Lilli Pro) on the device that holds your data, and you must sign in with the same account on both devices.

How do I add a child or another household member?

Child profiles: Settings → Add a child account. Other members: Household → Add a member. These are profiles inside your household; they don’t need their own sign-in.

I’m not getting notifications.

Open Settings → Notifications in the App to jump to your device’s notification settings and make sure Lilli: Clean Sweep is allowed.

Something is broken.

Email dustin.t.ellsworth@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
